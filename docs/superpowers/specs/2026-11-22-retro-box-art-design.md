# Retro box art for Spruce collections

## Why

Spruce (PyUI) shows custom collections as a plain list, like a ROM list, with a box-art picture beside the selected
entry. The Art Book Next Outline pictures are made for a theme's slanted system band (ES-DE, Knulli, ROCKNIX, muOS);
cut down to a 300x300 square (`art-spruce/`) they look out of place next to game covers. Spruce gets its own set: every
Outline picture redrawn as a 16-bit style pixel sprite on the front of a retro game box, with the art's title on the
box. Other frontends that list collections like ROMs can use the same set later; for now only `spruce` cards get
it. ES-DE and the others keep the Outline art unchanged.

## Goals

- One box picture per art id: the 81 in `art.json` plus `surprise` (82).
- Each box: the subject of that id's Outline picture as a hand-made 32x32 pixel sprite, centred and never cut off, with
  the art's title printed on the box in a pixel font and a badge in the footer.
- Generated by a script from source data in the repo, deterministically (same input, same bytes).
- Three sizes on the card, the way PyUI picks box art for its views, so the list never scales a big picture down on the
  device.

## Non-goals

- No change to the Outline art, `gen_theme_art.py` or what any other layout gets.
- No QOI for collections (see Constraints).
- No new card setting: the card's existing `theme` switch keeps covering collection art on Spruce.
- Nothing in storage.

## Anti-goals

- Anything that reads as a shrunk or pixelated Outline icon rather than a sprite.
- Real console branding: no logos, seals, slogans ("Blast Processing", "Official Seal") or a copy of any real box's
  trade dress (the Genesis grid, the SNES stripe). The frame is our own.
- A badge, title or sprite that touches or crowds the coloured picture area, or a sprite clipped at any edge.
- Art that changes between runs ("random" badges are seeded, never `random()` or Python's `hash()`).

## Constraints

- PyUI (spruceOS `main`, `App/PyUI/main-ui/menus/games/collections_menu.py`) loads `Collections/Imgs/<name>.png`, a
  hardcoded `.png`, and `GridOrListEntry.get_image_path_variant` swaps `Imgs` for `Imgs_med` / `Imgs_small` when that
  file exists (small falls back to med, then the full one). So the three sizes are PNG files of the same name in
  `Collections/Imgs/`, `Collections/Imgs_med/`, `Collections/Imgs_small/`. A `.qoi` would not be found.
- Sizes follow the game box-art tiers already used for Spruce: fit in 640x480, 400x300 and 200x200.
- Collection art is repo data (`src/sync_roms/data/themes/art-book-next/`), never storage.
- Pillow is already a runtime dependency; the generator may use it. Code runs on Python 3.12.
- Spruce devices in `devices.json` top out at 750x560 (Miyoo Mini Flip) and 720x720 (RG CubeXX); most are 640x480.

## Approaches considered

Box shape (user picked **A**, from mockups):

- **A. Tall portrait box** — title band on top, sunburst behind the sprite, spine edge on the left, footer badge.
  Matches the portrait covers in ROM lists.
- B. Wide landscape box — vertical title stripe, sprite in a framed window. Sits oddly beside portrait covers.
- C. Square cartridge — sprite on a label in a ridged shell. Least box-like.

Making the sprites (user picked **1**):

- **1. Hand-made 32x32 sprites stored as text grids**, one per id, drawn by a script into the box. The only way to get
  real 16-bit pixel art; one icon is fixed by editing one small file. Cost: 82 hand-made sprites.
- 2. Code-drawn sprites from shape rules (as in the mockup's sword). Faster for simple shapes, stiff for characters
  (Pikachu, Sonic, Yoshi), harder to tweak.
- 3. Pixelate the Outline drawings automatically. Fastest, but thin lines break into jaggies and colouring is flat.

## Design (as decided)

- Box A layout, as in the approved mockup (84x120 base pixels, drawn at 4x = 336x480 for the full size): dark title band
  on top, a thin stripe line under it, the art's colour band with a lighter sunburst behind the sprite, a darker spine on
  the left edge with a light highlight line, a dark footer, a thin dark outline round the box. The mockup's numbers, in
  base pixels (C = the box colour, `shade(C, f)` = each channel times f, clamped): title band `#14121C`, y 0..30 for two
  lines (it grows by one line height for a third); stripe lines at y 31, 33, 35 in `shade(C, 0.70 / 0.85 / 1.00)`;
  sunburst 16 rays, alternate rays `shade(C, 1.18)` on C, centred on the sprite; spine x 0..4 `shade(C, 0.55)` with a
  highlight line at x 5 `shade(C, 1.35)`; footer `#14121C`, the bottom 10 px in the mockup, made taller (about 12) so the
  badge has a clear pixel above it; box outline `#0C0A10`; sprite outline `#181020`; title text white and gold
  `#FAD048` on alternate lines, each with a 1 px `#181020` drop shadow.
- The box's colour is that id's band colour in the Outline set, so the two sets keep the same colour per collection.
- The sprite is the same subject as that id's Outline picture (the sword for `action-classics`, the heart for
  `favorites`, Pikachu for `pokemon`...). 32x32, drawn at 2x base pixels (8 screen pixels per sprite pixel at full
  size), centred in the picture area between the title band and the footer, with a clear margin on every side.
- Sprites use a shared 16-bit style palette: a dark outline colour plus colour ramps (highlight, base, shadow) so every
  sprite is shaded the same way, light from the top left.
- Title: the art's title from `art.json` (`surprise`: "Surprise"), never the collection's own name (user's call: the
  boxes are made once and committed, and sync only copies them; a collection named differently from its art shows the
  art's title), in capitals, in our own pixel font (not Pillow's
  built-in font). The font covers what titles use today: A–Z, 0–9, space, `&`, `'`, `-`, plus `!` and `:`. Long titles
  wrap at spaces onto up to three lines ("Dungeon Crawlers & Roguelikes" is the longest, 29 characters); the title band
  grows to fit and the picture area stays big enough for the sprite with its margin. Alternate lines may use white and
  gold as in the mockup.
- Badge: one per box from this list, chosen by a stable hash of the id (e.g. `hashlib.sha256`):
  `16-BIT`, `16 MEG`, `1-2 PLAYERS`, `1-4 PLAYERS`, `BATTERY BACKUP`, `STEREO SOUND`, `HI-SCORE`, `TURBO`, `NEW!`,
  `ARCADE HIT`, `PASSWORD SAVE`, `COLLECTOR'S`. One font for titles and badges, 4x5 glyphs with a 1 px gap (14
  characters across the ~74 usable base pixels); every badge fits one line (designer's call: `COLLECTOR'S EDITION`
  shortened to `COLLECTOR'S` to fit). Its colour is also picked by the hash from white, gold and the
  box colour lightened. The footer is tall enough that the badge never touches the coloured area.
- Three sizes per id, PNG: full 336x480 (fits 640x480), med 210x300 (fits 400x300), small 140x200 (fits 200x200).

## Calls left to the builder (marked as the designer's calls, correct them if wrong)

- File layout: sprites as `src/sync_roms/data/themes/art-book-next/sprites/<id>.txt` (32 lines of 32 characters, one
  character per palette entry, `.` transparent), the palette and pixel font as small JSON or text files beside them, the
  output in `art-box/Imgs/`, `art-box/Imgs_med/`, `art-box/Imgs_small/` (`<id>.png` each). The generator is
  `tools/gen_box_art.py [id ...]`, like the other art tools; its output is committed.
- Med and small are downscaled from the full size with a smooth filter (2.5x and the 140x200 fit aren't whole multiples
  of the base, so nearest-neighbour would give uneven pixels).
- Mixed pixel sizes are intended: sprites at 2x, frame and title at 1x base pixels, like real boxes where the lettering
  is finer than the art.
- `art-spruce/` and `tools/gen_spruce_art.py` are replaced and deleted: sync installs the three box sizes on Spruce
  instead. `themeart.usable` / `install_spruce` / `describe_spruce` / `_spruce_stale` write and remove all three folders
  (removing an owned collection's picture from each). Tests and `CLAUDE.md` (Theme art data, the Spruce line in "What
  sync puts on a card") are updated to match.
- Sprites are made and reviewed in batches of about 20 with a contact sheet of the finished boxes shown to the user
  after each batch; review images stay out of the commit. The theme-art checking rules in `CLAUDE.md` apply in spirit:
  look at every result, enlarged, before calling a batch done.
- Tests: every art id (and `surprise`) has a sprite of the right size using only palette characters, and three PNGs of
  the right sizes; the generator is deterministic (badge pick stable for an id); every title character is in the font;
  sync writes and removes the three folders on a Spruce card.
